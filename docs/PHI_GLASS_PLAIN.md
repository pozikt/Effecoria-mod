# Φ-Пустыня — Стеклянная Равнина

**Φ-Desert / Glass Plain** (`effecoria:phi_glass_plain`): high Φ_nature dunes of fused sand,
essonite dust barghans, and crystal pockets under the crust.

## Placement

TerraBlender region weight **8** replaces vanilla `minecraft:desert` in that slice.
Paired with Dead Wasteland (also weight **8**) so each claims ~**25%** of the regional
lottery; remaining deserts stay vanilla. Needs a **new world**.

`/locate biome effecoria:phi_glass_plain` → fly to **X Z** on the surface.

## Look

- `phi_glass` / `phi_glass_dune` crust (ochre–gold, night glow)
- `essonite_dust_block` underlayer and barghan mounds (quicksand)
- `phi_quartz` blobs + crystal cave pockets
- Ochre–teal horizon haze; thicker during Φ-storms

## Gameplay

| System | Behavior |
|--------|----------|
| Φ sample | Strong ambient (+ storm / noon bump) |
| Fire / lightning elemental | ×1.25 power |
| Water / ice / steam elemental | ×0.6 |
| Spatial | ×0.85; storm adds entropy + look jitter |
| Storms | Abrasion, hunger/weakness, Φ-lightning |
| Barghans | Sink + slow; deep bury damage |
| Mobs | Φ-scorpion (poison / Φ-burn sting) |

## Water

Biome has no lake/spring features. Runtime drying is **not** used (same policy as
Dead Wasteland for now).
