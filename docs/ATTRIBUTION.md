## Kenney (CC0)

Organic particle/icon polish may use crops inspired by or derived from free packs by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl):

- [Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack) (CC0)
- [Foliage Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/foliage-pack) (CC0)

Current Effecoria organic sprites are primarily generated by `scripts/generate_spell_assets.py` (PIL) to match Minecraft 16×16 / 64×64 pixel style. Attribution is retained for optional Kenney-sourced crops under `third_party/` (not required to ship).
