# Legalization — самозанятость (НПД)

Practical checklist for RU income from Boosty, Patreon, DLC sales, and server cosmetics.

**Not formal legal advice.** For edge cases, book 1 hour with a tax consultant.

## Golden rule

Do **not** register ИП or ООО “for later.”  
Register **НПД (самозанятость)** when the **first real payout** arrives (or 1–2 days before).

Opening a company with zero revenue burns money (fees, time, empty reporting). НПД registration is free.

---

## When to act

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| No income yet | Nothing tax-wise; build the mod |
| First Boosty/Patreon/server payout incoming | Register НПД → receive money → issue receipt |
| Unstable income, under ~100–150k ₽/mo average | Stay on НПД |
| Near annual НПД limit (~2.4M ₽ — verify for current year) | Plan switch to ИП |
| Need employees, B2B contracts, heavy acquiring | Consider ИП + УСН |

---

## Step-by-step — first payout

1. Install **«Мой налог»** (ФНС).
2. Register as самозанятый (passport / Госуслуги).
3. Add activity close to: *разработка ПО / продажа цифрового контента / информационные услуги* (pick what matches the UI list).
4. Receive payout to **your** card (not a friend’s).
5. In the app: create a **чек** for the amount received (income date = payout date).
6. Keep Boosty/Patreon payout screenshots + bank SMS/PDF in a folder `finance/YYYY-MM/`.
7. Pay the tax amount the app shows (monthly). Rates: typically **4%** from individuals, **6%** from legal entities — confirm how each platform’s payout is classified.

### Receipt description examples

- `Поддержка разработки мода Effecoria (Boosty)`
- `Patreon pledge — Effecoria`
- `Цифровой аддон School Codex`
- `Косметика официального сервера Effecoria`

## Monthly routine (15 minutes)

- [ ] Export payouts from Boosty / Patreon / donate shop
- [ ] Every inflow has a matching чек
- [ ] Tax paid if due
- [ ] Update simple ledger (date, source, gross, fee, net, чек id)

### Minimal ledger columns

`date | source | gross | platform_fee | net_received | tax_rate | cheque_id | notes`

Spreadsheet is enough. Separate “mod budget” from personal spending mentally or with tags.

---

## Foreign platforms (Patreon, etc.)

- Income is still declared under НПД when you receive it as a self-employed person in RF (follow current ФНС rules for foreign income).
- Price tiers with FX + bank fees in mind.
- Do not route money through third-party personal cards.

---

## Server donate

- Same: чек on money received.
- Sell cosmetics / QoL / seasons only — see [SERVER.md](SERVER.md).
- If using a payment aggregator, read whether they withhold tax; usually **you** still issue the чек.

---

## When to consider ИП

- Approaching НПД annual cap
- Hiring mods/builders with contracts
- Need settlement accounts / advertising as a business
- НПД activity list no longer fits

Typical next step: **ИП + УСН «доходы» 6%** (confirm region / year).  
Do **not** open ООО at creator start.

Leaving НПД / registering ИП: do it with a checklist from ФНС or an accountant — do not improvise mid-month without reading the transition rules.

---

## Explicit “do not”

- [ ] Pay for ООО / retainer accountant at ₽0 revenue
- [ ] Promise Boosty rewards you cannot ship
- [ ] Mix “I forgot to cheque” for months
- [ ] Sell Minecraft itself or violate Mojang EULA commercial rules — sell **your** content/services
