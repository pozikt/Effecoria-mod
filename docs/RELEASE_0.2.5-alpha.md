# Release notes — Effecoria `0.2.5-alpha` (Demo 5)

NeoForge **1.21.1** · jar: `effecoria-0.2.5-alpha.jar`

**Theme:** Stage II environment — biomes, weather, fauna, and living Φ/Ω zones.

## Highlights

- **Essence Plateau** — high-Φ peaks, flora, geysers, fog, cave treasures, Phi-water, and plateau fauna (larva, crystal crab, Eidos, Essence Wyvern)
- **Vitrified Wastes & Dead Wasteland** — glassed desert after a Φ-flash vs zero-Φ ash; golems, storms, shielding
- **Crystal Forest** — dense crystal canopy, mist/rain feel, storm screening under trees
- **Ω-Scar** — wound biome: razor blades, rotten moss, Ω-crystals, Eldritch blood puddles, Rotfang / Shade / Worm fauna, gravity & entropy pressure
- **Biome Φ/Ω weather** — storms, dew, and quieter UX (less whisper spam)
- **Mental servitude** — dig/haul servant orders with Ψ reserve like thralls
- **Wild mages** — hostile mobs can spawn as random-school casters
- **Essonite / Φ-chitin armor**, village Φ-alchemy machines, Whispering Spire landmark
- **Hyperspace** — survival physics, essentialization, Φ-thrust
